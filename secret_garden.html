The Secret Garden

Written by Frances Hodgson Burnett

About the Book

The Secret Garden is a classic children's novel about friendship, nature, healing, and personal change.

Summary

Mary Lennox is a lonely young girl who is sent to live with her uncle at a large estate in England after becoming orphaned.

While exploring the estate, Mary discovers a locked garden that has been neglected for many years. She becomes curious about its history and secretly begins bringing the garden back to life.

Mary also discovers her cousin Colin, a boy who has spent much of his life isolated inside the house. With the help of their friend Dickon, Mary and Colin spend time outdoors and begin changing their attitudes toward life.

The garden becomes a place where the children develop friendship, confidence, and a greater appreciation for nature and each other.
