The Diary of a Young Girl

Written by Anne Frank

About the Book

Anne Frank's diary records her thoughts, relationships, fears, hopes, and experiences while hiding during Nazi persecution.

Detailed Summary

Anne begins her diary as a young teenager and later continues writing while her family and others hide in a concealed space during World War II. Her entries describe ordinary teenage concerns alongside the extraordinary circumstances around her. She reflects on family relationships, personal growth, fear, hope, and her ambition to become a writer. The diary gives readers a personal perspective on a major historical tragedy.

What Makes This Book Interesting?

This title gives readers a chance to explore its central ideas through memorable people, events, and questions. It can also be used as a starting point for class discussion and personal reflection.

Main Themes

  • Hope
  • Family
  • History
  • Growing up

What You Can Learn

Readers can use the book to think more deeply about its themes, compare them with real-life experiences, and form their own informed opinions.
