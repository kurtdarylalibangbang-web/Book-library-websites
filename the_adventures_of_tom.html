The Adventures of Tom Sawyer

Written by Mark Twain

About the Book

The Adventures of Tom Sawyer follows a mischievous young boy growing up in a small town along the Mississippi River.

Summary

Tom Sawyer is an adventurous boy who often finds himself getting into trouble. He dislikes ordinary responsibilities and prefers exploring, playing, and creating exciting adventures with his friends.

Tom experiences many memorable events involving his friends, including Huckleberry Finn. He also develops feelings for Becky Thatcher and tries to impress her.

Tom and Huck become involved in a frightening situation after witnessing a crime. They struggle with fear and uncertainty while trying to decide what they should do.

Throughout the story, Tom learns about friendship, courage, responsibility, and the consequences of his actions.
