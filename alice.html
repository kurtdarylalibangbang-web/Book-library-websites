Alice's Adventures in Wonderland

Written by Lewis Carroll

About the Book

Alice's Adventures in Wonderland is a fantasy novel about a young girl named Alice who enters a strange and imaginative world filled with unusual characters and events.

Summary

Alice becomes curious when she sees a White Rabbit and follows it down a rabbit hole. She finds herself in a strange world called Wonderland.

In Wonderland, Alice meets unusual characters including the Cheshire Cat, the Mad Hatter, and the Queen of Hearts. She also experiences strange changes in her size and encounters situations that challenge her understanding of the world.

Alice travels through Wonderland while trying to understand its confusing rules and unpredictable inhabitants.

Eventually, Alice wakes from her strange adventure and realizes that her journey through Wonderland was a remarkable dream.
