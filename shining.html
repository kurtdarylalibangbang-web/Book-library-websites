The Shining

Written by Stephen King

About the Book

The Shining is a psychological horror novel by Stephen King. It follows Jack Torrance and his family as they spend the winter caring for the isolated Overlook Hotel.

Summary

Jack Torrance accepts a job as the winter caretaker of the Overlook Hotel in Colorado. He brings his wife Wendy and young son Danny with him, hoping the isolated environment will give him time to work on his writing.

Danny possesses a mysterious psychic ability that allows him to sense things that other people cannot. Soon after arriving at the hotel, he begins experiencing frightening visions and discovers that the building has a disturbing history.

As winter arrives, the family becomes trapped by heavy snow. Jack's behavior gradually changes as the influence of the hotel becomes stronger. The isolation and supernatural events create increasing tension within the family.

The story follows the family's struggle to survive while Danny tries to understand his unusual ability and the danger surrounding the hotel.
