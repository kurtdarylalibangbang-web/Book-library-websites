The Great Gatsby

Written by F. Scott Fitzgerald

About the Book

Nick Carraway becomes drawn into the world of Jay Gatsby, wealth, parties, lost love, and the American Dream.

Detailed Summary

Nick moves to Long Island during the Jazz Age and becomes neighbors with the mysterious millionaire Jay Gatsby. Gatsby's impressive parties hide a personal dream: reconnecting with Daisy Buchanan. As Nick watches Gatsby, Daisy, and Tom Buchanan, he sees how wealth and status can hide unhappiness and irresponsibility. The novel questions whether ambition and money can truly recreate the past or guarantee happiness.

What Makes This Book Interesting?

This title gives readers a chance to explore its central ideas through memorable people, events, and questions. It can also be used as a starting point for class discussion and personal reflection.

Main Themes

  • American Dream
  • Wealth
  • Love
  • Ambition

What You Can Learn

Readers can use the book to think more deeply about its themes, compare them with real-life experiences, and form their own informed opinions.
