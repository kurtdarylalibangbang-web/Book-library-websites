Man's Search for Meaning

Written by Viktor E. Frankl

About the Book

Viktor Frankl combines memoir and psychology to explore meaning, purpose, and the human response to extreme circumstances.

Detailed Summary

Frankl reflects on his experiences during the Holocaust and on the psychological effects of imprisonment. He considers why people respond differently to suffering and develops ideas that became part of logotherapy, an approach centered on the search for meaning. The book emphasizes purpose, responsibility, relationships, and the ways people may find meaning even when circumstances are difficult.

What Makes This Book Interesting?

This title gives readers a chance to explore its central ideas through memorable people, events, and questions. It can also be used as a starting point for class discussion and personal reflection.

Main Themes

  • Meaning
  • Purpose
  • Resilience
  • Responsibility

What You Can Learn

Readers can use the book to think more deeply about its themes, compare them with real-life experiences, and form their own informed opinions.
