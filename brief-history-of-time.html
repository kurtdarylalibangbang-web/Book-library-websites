A Brief History of Time

Written by Stephen Hawking

About the Book

An accessible introduction to major questions about the universe, including space, time, gravity, stars, and black holes.

Detailed Summary

Stephen Hawking introduces readers to major ideas in cosmology and physics. The book discusses the nature of space and time, the development of the universe, black holes, and scientific attempts to understand fundamental questions. It presents complex concepts in a way intended for general readers.

Main Themes

  • Science
  • Space
  • Time
  • The universe

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
