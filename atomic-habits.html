Atomic Habits

Written by James Clear

About the Book

A practical guide about building useful habits, reducing unhelpful patterns, and making small improvements consistently.

Detailed Summary

James Clear explains how habits develop and how small changes can accumulate over time. The book presents ideas such as making good habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying. It encourages readers to focus on systems and repeated actions rather than relying only on motivation.

Main Themes

  • Habits
  • Consistency
  • Growth
  • Self-improvement

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
