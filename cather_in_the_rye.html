The Catcher in the Rye

Written by J.D. Salinger

About the Book

The Catcher in the Rye follows Holden Caulfield, a teenager who struggles with school, relationships, growing up, and his view of the adult world.

Summary

Holden Caulfield leaves his school in Pennsylvania after learning that he has been expelled. Instead of immediately returning home, he spends several days in New York City.

During his time in the city, Holden meets different people and reflects on his relationships, school experiences, and his changing feelings about growing up.

Holden often feels disconnected from the people around him. His conversations and experiences reveal his desire to protect innocence while struggling to understand the complicated world of adults.

The story focuses on identity, loneliness, growing up, innocence, and the difficulties of moving from childhood toward adulthood.
