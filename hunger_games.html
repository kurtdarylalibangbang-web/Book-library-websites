The Hunger Games

Written by Suzanne Collins

About the Book

The Hunger Games is a dystopian novel about Katniss Everdeen, a teenager living in the nation of Panem.

Summary

Katniss lives with her family in District 12, one of the districts controlled by the Capitol. Each year, young people from the districts are selected to participate in the Hunger Games.

When her younger sister is selected, Katniss volunteers to take her place. She is sent to the Capitol with Peeta Mellark, another representative from her district.

Katniss and Peeta must survive the dangerous competition while being watched by the entire nation. They also have to deal with the political power of the Capitol.

Katniss's decisions begin to challenge the system that controls the districts. The story explores courage, inequality, survival, friendship, and resistance.
