The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe

Written by C.S. Lewis

About the Book

Four siblings discover a magical world through a wardrobe and become involved in a struggle between good and evil.

Detailed Summary

Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy enter Narnia, a magical land trapped in a long winter. They meet allies and face the White Witch while learning about courage, loyalty, sacrifice, and responsibility. Their adventure transforms them as they become part of a larger conflict.

Main Themes

  • Courage
  • Good and evil
  • Family
  • Sacrifice

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
