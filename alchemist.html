The Alchemist

Written by Paulo Coelho

About the Book

The Alchemist follows Santiago, a young shepherd who dreams about discovering treasure. His journey becomes a search for purpose, courage, and personal fulfillment.

Summary

Santiago is a young shepherd from Spain who repeatedly dreams about treasure near the Egyptian pyramids. After meeting people who encourage him to follow his dream, he decides to begin a journey.

Santiago travels across different places and meets people who teach him important lessons about life, opportunity, and following his goals.

During his journey, Santiago works, learns about the world, and meets an alchemist who helps him understand the importance of listening to his heart and learning from his experiences.

Santiago eventually reaches the place connected to his dream. His journey teaches him that the search for a goal can be just as meaningful as reaching the goal itself.
