FICTION

1984

Written by George Orwell

About the Book

A dystopian story about Winston Smith, a man living under a government that controls information, history, and independent thought.

Detailed Summary

Winston secretly questions the Party's control of society. His work rewriting records makes him aware of how truth can be manipulated. As he searches for personal freedom and becomes close to Julia, he discovers how deeply the Party can reach into people's private lives and beliefs. The novel examines surveillance, propaganda, censorship, language, fear, and the importance of independent thought.

What Makes This Book Interesting?

This title gives readers a chance to explore its central ideas through memorable people, events, and questions. It can also be used as a starting point for class discussion and personal reflection.

Main Themes

  • Government control
  • Surveillance
  • Propaganda
  • Freedom of thought

What You Can Learn

Readers can use the book to think more deeply about its themes, compare them with real-life experiences, and form their own informed opinions.
