FICTION

Pride and Prejudice

Written by Jane Austen

About the Book

A classic romance and social comedy about Elizabeth Bennet, Mr. Darcy, family expectations, and first impressions.

Detailed Summary

Elizabeth Bennet navigates family pressures and the social expectations of her community while developing a complicated relationship with the proud and reserved Mr. Darcy. Misunderstandings and changing impressions force both characters to reconsider their judgments of one another. The story explores love, class, reputation, family, and personal growth.

Main Themes

  • Love
  • Class
  • Family
  • Personal growth

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
