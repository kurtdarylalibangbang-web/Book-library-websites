Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Written by J.K. Rowling

About the Book

Harry Potter discovers that he is a wizard and begins a new life at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

Detailed Summary

Harry learns about his magical heritage and travels to Hogwarts, where he meets Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. During his first year, the three friends explore the school, learn magic, and investigate a mystery involving a valuable magical object. Their experiences teach them about friendship, courage, loyalty, identity, and making responsible choices.

What Makes This Book Interesting?

This title gives readers a chance to explore its central ideas through memorable people, events, and questions. It can also be used as a starting point for class discussion and personal reflection.

Main Themes

  • Friendship
  • Courage
  • Identity
  • Belonging

What You Can Learn

Readers can use the book to think more deeply about its themes, compare them with real-life experiences, and form their own informed opinions.
