FICTION

The Little Prince

Written by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

About the Book

A poetic tale about a young traveler who visits different worlds and learns about friendship, love, responsibility, and seeing beyond appearances.

Detailed Summary

The Little Prince leaves his small asteroid and encounters unusual adults on different planets before reaching Earth. His conversations with a pilot and a fox lead him to reflect on relationships, responsibility, loneliness, and what gives life meaning. The story encourages readers to look beyond surface appearances.

Main Themes

  • Friendship
  • Responsibility
  • Love
  • Perspective

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
