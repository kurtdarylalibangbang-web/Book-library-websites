The Hobbit

Written by J.R.R. Tolkien

About the Book

Bilbo Baggins leaves his comfortable home and joins a company of dwarves on an unexpected adventure.

Detailed Summary

Bilbo begins as a quiet hobbit who prefers a peaceful life. After joining Thorin Oakenshield and the dwarves, he travels through dangerous lands and encounters challenges that require courage and cleverness. His journey changes his understanding of himself and shows how an unlikely hero can grow through experience.

Main Themes

  • Adventure
  • Courage
  • Friendship
  • Growth

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
