The Chronicles of Narnia

Written by C.S. Lewis

About the Book

The Chronicles of Narnia is a fantasy series about children who discover the magical world of Narnia and become involved in its struggles and adventures.

Summary

The stories follow different children who enter the magical world of Narnia through extraordinary circumstances. In this world, animals can speak and magical creatures live alongside humans.

The children meet important characters such as Aslan and become involved in conflicts between good and evil. They face challenges that require courage, loyalty, and teamwork.

Across their adventures, the characters learn important lessons about friendship, sacrifice, responsibility, and choosing what is right.
