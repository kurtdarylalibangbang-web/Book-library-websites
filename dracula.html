Dracula

Written by Bram Stoker

About the Book

Dracula is a Gothic horror novel by Bram Stoker. It tells the story of Count Dracula and the people who discover his supernatural activities.

Summary

Jonathan Harker travels to Transylvania to help Count Dracula with a property transaction. While staying at Dracula's castle, Jonathan begins to realize that his host is mysterious and dangerous.

Dracula eventually travels to England, where strange events begin to occur. Lucy Westenra becomes seriously ill, and her friends become concerned about the unusual circumstances surrounding her condition.

Professor Abraham Van Helsing investigates the strange events and realizes that they are connected to a vampire. Jonathan, Mina, Van Helsing, and their allies work together to understand Dracula and stop his plans.

The novel follows their dangerous struggle against Dracula while exploring fear, courage, friendship, and the conflict between supernatural forces and ordinary people.
