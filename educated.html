Educated

Written by Tara Westover

About the Book

A memoir about education, family, self-discovery, and the process of building a life through learning.

Detailed Summary

Tara Westover describes growing up in a family that had limited contact with formal education. As she becomes interested in learning, education opens opportunities that lead her to new environments and perspectives. Her memoir explores family relationships, identity, independence, and the transformative power of education.

Main Themes

  • Education
  • Identity
  • Family
  • Independence

What You Can Learn

This book gives readers ideas and experiences they can reflect on, discuss, and connect with their own understanding of people, society, learning, or life.
